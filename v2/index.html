YIFacebook · May 2019 to Dec 2021 · Founder

Yogic Insights: zero to 1.18M followers

A page I started myself, a clear posting plan, and 87M+ views on 149 videos.

iAn independent, unofficial page. It shares short videos of Sadhguru's teachings. It is not linked to Sadhguru or Isha Foundation.

Started at 0 followers. Short videos about spirituality and wellness. I did every part myself.

AThe challenge

Grow an audience from nothing. No followers. No brand behind the page. Only short videos to work with.

BWhat I did

The plan

  • One type of video, again and again. Short clips with subtitles, made for the feed.
  • Post more often to grow. I went from 0‑2 posts a month in 2019 to up to 21 a month in 2020.
  • Two posting times. 7 in 10 videos went out at 13:00-15:00 or 20:00-22:00 IST.
  • Look at the typical video, not one big hit. I count how many videos pass 100K, not just the top one.

The work

  • 179 posts and 149 different videos, from 13 May 2019 to 28 Dec 2021.
  • 146 posts in the 10 months from Mar to Dec 2020.
  • I did every step myself. I picked and edited the clips and added subtitles. I made the titles, thumbnails and captions, posted, and read the numbers.

The playbook

How I grow an audience

Six habits. My own numbers back up each one.

  1. 01

    How often

    Post often to grow.

    I took Yogic Insights from 0‑2 posts a month to up to 21. Both 13M-view months came with 18 to 20 posts a month. In 2021 I posted less, and views fell below 3M a month.

    Apr 2020 · 18 posts · 13.2M views
  2. 02

    Timing

    Post when your audience is scrolling.

    7 in 10 of my videos went out at two times: 13:00-15:00 and 20:00-22:00 IST. A typical 13:00 video got 462K views. That is 1.7x the page's typical video.

    13:00 IST · typical video 462K · 19 videos
  3. 03

    Hooks

    Grab them fast. Then keep them watching.

    The first few seconds decide if people keep watching. My Shorts shown here are 59 to 71 seconds long. 3 of the 6 passed 1M views.

    Took My Family to Mumbai · 2.62M
  4. 04

    Titles

    Get the title and thumbnail right.

    The title, thumbnail, caption and crop are part of the edit. They are not an extra job for later. On the Keerthi brand, I did posting, titles, thumbnails and captions, and looked after the channel. I did this along with the editing.

    Core team · 2.96M subscribers · BRAND TOTAL
  5. 05

    AI

    Let AI make the shots. Keep the choices human.

    I use Runway, Kling AI and Google Flow for POV shots and B-roll. I finish them in Premiere Pro and After Effects. In one 26-minute documentary, about 60% of the POV shots and B-roll were made with AI.

    I Compared India & America's Middle Class
  6. 06

    Typical video

    Look at the typical video, then improve.

    One viral post proves nothing. 125 of my 149 Yogic Insights videos passed 100K views. A typical video got 268K views. That is the number I watch.

    125 of 149 past 100K
